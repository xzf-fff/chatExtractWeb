Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22. In one line: your conversations never leave your browser; ads on the page are served by Google AdSense and may use cookies.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts: no sign-up, no email or phone number collected.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, no beacons).
  • We do not collect the conversations, images or ZIP files you select.

How your files are handled

Both tools are pure front-end pages. The export you drop in is read into memory via the browser's File API; parsing, validation, merging and packing all happen locally. The only write to disk is your browser saving the ZIP when you click “Download”. Nothing is uploaded.

Closing or reloading the page discards everything in memory, including temporary preview URLs. Conversation content is never stored in IndexedDB or localStorage (localStorage only remembers your language and theme preference).

Ads and cookies

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • The ad script has no access to the files you select; it only requests ad creatives from Google.

The two tool pages (subset extractor, version merger) do not load the ad script; ads appear only on the home page, the guide, the troubleshooting page and this page.

See Google's explanation of how it uses data from partner sites.

Hosting and access logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any website, the server logs IP address, user agent, request path and timestamp for security and debugging. Those logs never contain the conversations you select, because the page never uploads them.

Third-party links

The site links to third parties such as chat-demo and GitHub, each with its own privacy policy. Links found inside your conversations are only rendered as http(s) and are never opened automatically.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads: Google Ads Settings.
  • Block the ad script with a browser extension or ad blocker; the tools do not depend on it, so everything keeps working.
  • Use it fully offline: clone the repository and serve it locally; no external requests are made.

Children

The site is intended for adults, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

Updates change the “last updated” date at the top of this page; material changes to data handling will be announced on the home page.
