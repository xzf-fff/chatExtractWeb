DeepSeek 导出常见问题DeepSeek export FAQ

关于导出的 10 个高频问题，每个都给短答案 + 指向细节页。Ten frequently asked questions, each with a short answer and a pointer to the details.

1. 导出的数据里包含什么？1. What is inside?

manifest（格式声明）、可选的 user.json（账号信息）、会话索引、每个会话的完整消息树，以及 _files/ 下的图片附件。不含账号密码。结构详见 导出包结构。A manifest, optional user.json, the index, every conversation's message tree and the images under _files/. No passwords. See the structure article.

2. 已删除的对话会出现在导出里吗？2. Do deleted conversations appear?

不会——导出只包含当时的现存会话。但要注意：删过消息后，**后续消息 id 会整体前移**，所以两份导出的 id 可能对不上（见 合并）。No — only what exists at export time. Note, however, that deleting messages shifts later ids, so two exports may disagree on ids (see merging).

3. 能只导出其中几组对话吗？3. Can I export only a few conversations?

官方导出是全量的。要只留几组，用 子集提取器 勾选后重新打包——它会同时裁剪会话和索引。步骤见 只保留部分对话。The official export is all-or-nothing. To keep a few, use the subset extractor, which prunes sessions and the index together. Steps in keeping a subset.

4. 导出格式会变吗？4. Can the format change?

目前是 export_format_version: 3。如果官方升级格式，本工具也需要同步调整；导入时若版本不匹配会直接报错。It is currently export_format_version: 3. If the format changes, these tools would need updating too; a mismatch fails the import immediately.

5. 为什么导入 chat-demo 会覆盖已有对话？5. Why does importing overwrite?

导入是「整包替换」：先清空本地对话与附件，再写入包内。导入前先在 demo 里「导出所有历史对话」备份。Import replaces everything: local conversations and attachments are cleared, then the package is written. Export your history inside the demo first.

6. 导入后图片为什么不显示？6. Why are images missing after import?

附件没跟着打包，或 export_path 与实际路径不一致。保持 chat_sessions/<id>_files/ 原样复制即可，详见 图片与附件。Attachments weren't packed, or export_path doesn't match. Copy chat_sessions/<id>_files/ verbatim — see images and attachments.

7. 两份导出可以合并吗？7. Can two exports be merged?

可以。按会话 id 分组、按消息对齐取并集，内容不同的逐条选择。细节见 两次导出怎么合并。Yes. Group by conversation id, align by message, union them, and resolve conflicts one by one. See merging two exports.

8. 导出的 ZIP 能直接打开查看吗？8. Can I just open the ZIP?

能解压，但 JSON 是给程序读的。想浏览对话，导入 chat-demo 更实际。You can unzip it, but the JSON is machine-oriented. Importing into chat-demo is the practical way to browse.

9. 多久导出一次比较好？9. How often should I export?

每月一次，加上清理对话、换设备、重要合并之前各一次。备份成本很低，丢数据成本很高；见 备份与迁移。Monthly, plus before cleaning up conversations, switching devices or an important merge. See backup and migration.

10. 导出数据安全吗？10. Is the export safe?

导出包里有完整对话和截图，本身就等于一份聊天记录存档。建议用本地工具处理、分享前裁剪、不要上传到公共网盘；见 本地 vs 网盘。The package is a full chat archive including screenshots. Process it locally, trim before sharing, and avoid public drives — see local vs cloud.
