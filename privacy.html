隐私政策Privacy policy

最后更新：2026-11-22。一句话：你的对话数据不会离开你的浏览器；页面上的广告由 Google AdSense 提供，可能使用 Cookie。Last updated: 2026-11-22. In one line: your conversations never leave your browser; ads on the page are served by Google AdSense and may use cookies.

我们不收集什么What we don't collect

  • 没有账号系统，不需要注册、不收集邮箱或手机号。No accounts: no sign-up, no email or phone number collected.
  • 没有统计/分析脚本（没有 Google Analytics、没有埋点）。No analytics or tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, no beacons).
  • 不收集你选择的对话内容、图片或生成的 ZIP。We do not collect the conversations, images or ZIP files you select.

你的文件如何被处理How your files are handled

两个工具都是纯前端页面。你拖入的导出包只被浏览器通过 File API 读取到内存中，解析、校验、合并与打包全部在本地完成；唯一写出磁盘的动作是你在点「下载」时浏览器保存 ZIP。整个过程没有任何上传请求。Both tools are pure front-end pages. The export you drop in is read into memory via the browser's File API; parsing, validation, merging and packing all happen locally. The only write to disk is your browser saving the ZIP when you click “Download”. Nothing is uploaded.

关闭页面或刷新后，内存中的数据和临时预览地址都会被丢弃；站点不使用 IndexedDB、localStorage 保存对话内容（localStorage 只存“语言/主题”这两个偏好）。Closing or reloading the page discards everything in memory, including temporary preview URLs. Conversation content is never stored in IndexedDB or localStorage (localStorage only remembers your language and theme preference).

广告与 CookieAds and cookies

本站通过 Google AdSense 展示广告。第三方供应商（包括 Google）会使用 Cookie，根据你此前对本站或其他网站的访问来投放广告。This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google 使用广告 Cookie，使其与合作伙伴能根据你访问本站和/或其他网站的情况向你投放广告。Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites.
  • 你可以访问 Google 广告设置 关闭个性化广告。You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • 也可以访问 aboutads.info 关闭部分第三方供应商的广告 Cookie。You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • 广告脚本接触不到你选择的文件内容——它只负责向 Google 请求广告素材。The ad script has no access to the files you select; it only requests ad creatives from Google.

两个工具页（子集提取、版本合并）不加载广告脚本，广告只出现在首页、使用指南、报错排查和本页。The two tool pages (subset extractor, version merger) do not load the ad script; ads appear only on the home page, the guide, the troubleshooting page and this page.

关于 Google 如何在合作伙伴网站使用数据，见 Google 的相关说明。See Google's explanation of how it uses data from partner sites.

托管与访问日志Hosting and access logs

本站托管在 Vercel。与其任何网站一样，服务器在处理请求时会记录 IP 地址、User-Agent、请求路径与时间戳，用于安全防护与故障排查。这些日志不包含你选择的对话内容（页面本身不上传文件）。The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any website, the server logs IP address, user agent, request path and timestamp for security and debugging. Those logs never contain the conversations you select, because the page never uploads them.

第三方链接Third-party links

页面中可能链接到 chat-demo、GitHub 等第三方站点，它们各自有自己的隐私政策，本站无法控制。对话数据里出现的链接在预览中只允许 http(s)，且不会被自动打开。The site links to third parties such as chat-demo and GitHub, each with its own privacy policy. Links found inside your conversations are only rendered as http(s) and are never opened automatically.

你的选择Your choices

  • 关闭个性化广告：Google 广告设置。Turn off personalised ads: Google Ads Settings.
  • 阻止广告脚本：使用浏览器扩展或广告拦截器；工具本身不依赖广告脚本，拦截后功能完全不受影响。Block the ad script with a browser extension or ad blocker; the tools do not depend on it, so everything keeps working.
  • 完全离线使用：把仓库下载到本地，用任意静态服务器打开，此时没有任何外部请求。Use it fully offline: clone the repository and serve it locally; no external requests are made.

儿童隐私Children

本站面向成年人使用，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。The site is intended for adults, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

政策变更Changes

政策如有更新，会修改本页顶部的「最后更新」日期；涉及数据处理方式的重大变化会在首页提示。Updates change the “last updated” date at the top of this page; material changes to data handling will be announced on the home page.
